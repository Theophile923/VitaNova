Privacy Policy

VitaNova · Last updated: November 5, 2026

VitaNova is a set of planning tools (SmartBiz, LegacyVault, BuildSmart) for Pi Network Pioneers, operated by Theophile HAKIZIMANA (Rwanda). We collect as little data as possible. This page explains exactly what is used and why.

1. What we collect

  • Your Pi username. When you sign in with Pi Authentication, we receive your Pi username (and, only where payment is offered, permission to request a payment). We do not receive your password, wallet keys, email address or phone number.
  • Payment references. If you pay in Pi, our payment server receives the payment identifier and the transaction ID so it can confirm the payment with Pi. We do not keep a database of payments.

2. What stays on your device

  • The information you type into the tools (for example business plans, succession drafts, building quotes, saved projects) is stored in your browser on your own device (local storage). It is not sent to our servers.
  • If you pay to unlock a full report, a marker containing the payment transaction ID is saved on your device so the report stays unlocked.
  • You can delete all of this at any time with the “New” button inside a tool or by clearing your browser data.

3. Third-party services

  • Pi Network (Pi Browser, Pi SDK, Pi servers) handles sign-in and payments, under Pi’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Exchange-rate service. BuildSmart requests the USD/FRW exchange rate from api.exchangerate-api.com. That service can see your IP address like any website you visit. No project data is sent.
  • Hosting. The site and its payment server are hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address and time of request).
  • LegacyVault. The draft deed you prepare (names of heirs, estate, clauses) stays on your device. VitaNova does not send it to any server or storage network. If you print or share the document, you control where it goes.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking or analytics scripts, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account other than Pi Authentication. No email or phone collection.

5. Your choices and contact

You can stop using VitaNova at any time and erase local data as described above. For any privacy question or request, write to theophileh56@gmail.com.

Important. VitaNova gives educational and indicative estimates only. It is not legal, financial, medical or engineering advice. USD or FRW amounts shown in the tools are unofficial estimates; Pi has no official market price.
